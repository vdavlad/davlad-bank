Founding Story
Davlad Bank is not a licensed financial institution. It is a conceptual project, a design experiment, and a long-running personal idea shaped by curiosity about finance, structure, and systems. Its origins go back to around 2005. While spending time at his grandparents’ home, surrounded by documents, papers, an old computer, and a calculator, the future founder developed an early fascination with financial processes and institutional structure. During that time, an initial concept emerged under the name “Bank Krot” — a name chosen without realizing that it might sound somewhat questionable in a financial context. The idea remained dormant for many years. In early 2026, it unexpectedly returned — this time with a more self-aware and ironic angle: the concept of a fictional bank with ambition, visual identity, structured products, and no actual liquidity behind it. What started as a joke quickly evolved into interface sketches, product ideas, and a growing interest in building something that feels real, even if it is not. Today, Davlad Bank exists somewhere between fiction and structure. It serves as a personal project, a creative outlet, and a learning platform for exploring financial logic, product design, and digital presentation. The project will continue to evolve over time — with new features, new tools, and new ideas — at least until the CEO resolves his own liquidity situation. Until then, the bank remains: ambitious in presentation, limited in liquidity, and ongoing by design.
