Security Notice

How to recognize a bank that is not real

Unrealistic promises

Be cautious when a “bank” promises guaranteed wealth, instant approval, or returns that sound detached from reality.

Unclear identity

A real institution should have a transparent identity, a consistent public presence, and clearly stated ownership or structure.

Pressure and urgency

Fraudulent actors often create artificial urgency, push immediate payments, or discourage proper verification.
